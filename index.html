Guptlekh

A quiet bench for classroom ciphers. The outer ring is plaintext; the inner ring is ciphertext. Turn the disk, watch a letter wrap past Z, then ask what an unknown sample might be.

What is this text?

Paste unknown ciphertext, a hash, or encoded data. Guptlekh ranks every format it can actually see — encodings, hash lengths, PEM/PGP wrappers, and classroom ciphers. AES-class algorithms stay a family guess: they are designed to look like noise.
